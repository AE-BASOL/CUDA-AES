<threat_model>
T-BENCH-01: Benchmark result contributions can omit raw artifacts or environment details, making results unreproducible.
Mitigation: The canonical checklist must require raw CSV files, generated summary, exact commands, commit hash, and hardware/software environment.

T-BENCH-02: Duplicated checklist text can drift across CONTRIBUTING.md, docs, and templates.
Mitigation: Keep detailed requirements in `docs/benchmark-result-contributions.md` and link to it from entry points.

T-BENCH-03: Contributors can submit universal ranking or "fastest" claims without controlled evidence.
Mitigation: Require claims-policy acknowledgement and frame accepted results as environment-specific measurements.
</threat_model>
